## Detailed description

**2izi Screenshot** captures a full page or a selected area and saves it
straight to a JPG file — nothing else opens.

**How it works**
- Click the toolbar icon
- Choose **Full page** to capture the entire scrollable page, stitched
  together automatically
- Choose **Select area** to drag a rectangle exactly like the native
  macOS screenshot tool — crosshair cursor, live size readout, Esc to cancel
- The JPG lands in your Downloads folder immediately. No preview tab, no
  editor window, no cloud upload.

**Why it's different**
Most screenshot extensions open a new tab, an editor, or a "review before
saving" screen. 2izi Screenshot skips all of that — one click in, one file
out.
